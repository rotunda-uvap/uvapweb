Andy joined UVA Press in 2022, having previously served as managing editor at Liturgical Press and adjunct professor in theology and religious studies at the College of St. Benedict and St. John’s University and the University of Lynchburg. He earned his MPhil at the University of St. Andrews and his PhD in systematic theology from the University of St. Michael’s College, Toronto. Andy acquires books for the following series: Studies in Religion and Culture, American Spirituality, and Religious Freedom and Public Dialogue: A Robert Nusbaum Center Series.

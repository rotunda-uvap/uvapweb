I acquire books in business and economics for the Darden Business Publishing (DBP) imprint of the UVA Press. I am particularly interested in books that translate thought-leading ideas into practice. The ideal book project will inspire responsible business leaders by analyzing consequential problems and issues and suggesting practical, innovative solutions. I aim for a wide range of ideas in the imprint, as business touches so many aspects of life and work.

I hold a BA in religious studies from Yale University and an MA in English from the University of Virginia. Prior to joining the Darden School, I worked at The Washington Papers. In 2018, I joined Darden Business Publishing, where my copyediting responsibilities soon expanded to include developmental and production editing. I oversaw the inaugural volumes of our imprint with UVA Press, and in 2025, I began as an acquisitions editor.
